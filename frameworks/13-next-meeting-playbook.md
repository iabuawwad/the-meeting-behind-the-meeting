# 13 - Next Meeting Playbook

Use this framework to convert analysis into preparation for the next interaction.

## What to Remember

Capture:

- Confirmed decisions.
- Unresolved questions.
- Stated concerns.
- Commitments made.
- Relationship or alignment risks.
- Topics that triggered confusion or disagreement.

Keep each item tied to transcript evidence.

## What to Confirm

Confirm:

- Decision status.
- Owner.
- Deadline.
- Output criteria.
- Dependencies.
- Approval path.
- Whether absent stakeholders need input.

Use direct wording:

- "Can we confirm that X is the decision, Y owns it, and Z is due by DATE?"

## Who to Align With

Identify people or groups to align with before the next meeting:

- Decision maker
- Implementation owner
- Blocker owner
- Dissenting stakeholder
- Absent affected stakeholder
- Customer, vendor, or partner contact

For each:

- Why alignment matters
- What to clarify
- Best evidence from the transcript

## What Not to Say

Avoid:

- Accusations about intent.
- Claims that someone secretly meant something.
- Overconfident tone or emotion claims.
- Reopening settled decisions without new evidence.
- Publicly cornering a participant on an ambiguous transcript point.
- Presenting hypotheses as facts.

Use instead:

- "I want to check my read of the discussion."
- "The transcript leaves this unclear."
- "Can we confirm the owner and output?"

## Questions to Ask

Question types:

- Clarifying: "What does done look like?"
- Ownership: "Who is directly accountable for this?"
- Risk: "What would block this by next week?"
- Alignment: "Does anyone see this differently?"
- Evidence: "What data would change the decision?"
- Decision: "Are we deciding this now or deferring it?"
- Relationship: "What do we need from each other to keep this on track?"

## Likely Objections

Identify likely objections from transcript evidence:

- Timeline concern
- Budget concern
- Scope concern
- Authority concern
- Technical feasibility concern
- Legal or compliance concern
- Customer or stakeholder impact concern
- Process burden concern

For each objection:

- Evidence
- Person or group likely to raise it
- Best response
- Needed proof or clarification

## Recommended Opening

Use an opening that reduces ambiguity:

```text
I want to start by confirming what I heard from the last discussion: the decision, the owner, and the open questions. If anything is wrong, let us correct it before we move into new items.
```

Adapt the wording to the meeting context.

## Recommended Closing

Use a closing that locks accountability:

```text
Before we close, can we confirm the decisions made today, each owner, the expected output, and the deadline? I will send the written recap after this.
```

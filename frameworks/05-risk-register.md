# 05 - Risk Register

Use this framework to convert transcript evidence into a practical risk register. Risks must be tied to facts, signals, and confidence.

## Risk Fields

For each risk, capture:

- Risk type
- Evidence
- Signal
- Possible impact
- Likelihood: high, medium, or low
- Severity: high, medium, or low
- Confidence: high, medium, or low
- Owner if known
- Mitigation or next action

## Risk Types

### Operational Risk

Execution, staffing, process, tooling, handoff, or workflow risk.

Signals:

- Unclear owners
- Process gaps
- Capacity concerns
- Repeated blockers

### Legal Risk

Contract, liability, claims, approval, or legal review risk.

Signals:

- Unreviewed terms
- Informal commitments
- Disputed obligations
- Requests to bypass legal process

### Compliance Risk

Regulatory, policy, audit, data handling, or required-control risk.

Signals:

- Missing approvals
- Incomplete records
- Unclear policy ownership
- Exceptions without documentation

### Reputation Risk

Risk to trust, credibility, customer perception, partner confidence, or public standing.

Signals:

- Missed commitments
- Inconsistent messaging
- Stakeholder frustration
- Repeated expectation resets

### Delivery Risk

Schedule, scope, quality, dependency, or milestone risk.

Signals:

- Unresolved blockers
- Ambiguous scope
- Compressed timelines
- No acceptance criteria

### Relationship Risk

Risk to working relationships, stakeholder alignment, or future cooperation.

Signals:

- Defensiveness
- Repeated disagreement
- Unanswered concerns
- Lack of reciprocity or follow-through

### Financial Risk

Budget, revenue, cost, pricing, payment, or resourcing risk.

Signals:

- Budget uncertainty
- Scope without cost clarity
- Payment or renewal concerns
- Unfunded commitments

### Security Risk

Access, data exposure, system integrity, confidentiality, or incident risk.

Signals:

- Sensitive data handling
- Unclear access controls
- Incident references
- Security exceptions or unresolved vulnerabilities

### Decision Risk

Risk that a decision is wrong, premature, delayed, unsupported, or not owned.

Signals:

- Decision without evidence
- Authority ambiguity
- Deferred tradeoffs
- No decision despite urgency

### Communication Risk

Risk caused by unclear, inconsistent, incomplete, or poorly routed communication.

Signals:

- Different participants understand different outcomes
- Action items are implied rather than stated
- Missing written confirmation
- Important stakeholders absent from the loop

## Public-Safe Rule

Do not turn risk signals into accusations. State what could go wrong, what evidence supports the concern, and what confirmation or mitigation is needed.

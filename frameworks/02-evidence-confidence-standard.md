# 02 - Evidence Confidence Standard

Every advanced claim must follow this structure. Advanced claims include implied meaning, risk, trust, influence, psychological safety, communication style, tone, and next-meeting predictions.

## Claim Structure

### Fact

What the transcript directly supports.

Requirements:

- Cite speaker, quote, timestamp, or clear transcript location when available.
- Do not add motive or emotion.
- Keep the wording close to the source.

Example:

- "Speaker A asked for a revised timeline after Speaker B said the current date was at risk."

### Signal

The observable pattern that may matter.

Requirements:

- Link the signal to one or more facts.
- Describe behavior, not personality.
- State whether the signal is strong, moderate, or weak.

Example:

- "The timeline was challenged twice and no owner accepted the next step."

### Interpretation

The cautious meaning the signal may suggest.

Requirements:

- Frame as "may indicate," "could suggest," or "likely means" only when confidence supports it.
- Do not present private intent as known.
- Do not diagnose, label character, or claim deception.

Example:

- "This may indicate schedule risk is recognized but not yet owned."

### Alternative Explanation

A plausible non-problematic or different explanation.

Requirements:

- Include at least one alternative for medium or high-impact claims.
- Consider transcript gaps, role expectations, time pressure, agenda constraints, or missing context.

Example:

- "The owner may have been assigned outside the captured transcript."

### Confidence

Rate confidence as high, medium, or low.

Guidance:

- High: multiple direct facts support the claim and transcript quality is strong.
- Medium: at least one direct fact supports the claim, but context is incomplete.
- Low: weak evidence, incomplete transcript, or several plausible alternatives.

### Recommended Action

Translate the claim into a practical next step.

Requirements:

- Prefer confirmation, clarification, or risk reduction.
- Do not recommend manipulation.
- Make the action proportionate to confidence.

Example:

- "Confirm the delivery owner and revised timeline in writing before the next meeting."

## Required Format

Use this format for advanced claims:

```markdown
### Claim

- Fact:
- Signal:
- Interpretation:
- Alternative explanation:
- Confidence:
- Recommended action:
```

## Enforcement

If the agent cannot fill the structure, it must downgrade the claim, ask for more context, or omit the claim.

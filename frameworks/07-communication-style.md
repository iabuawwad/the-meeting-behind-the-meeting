# 07 - Communication Style

Use this framework to map observable communication behavior. Do not label personality, identity, or fixed character.

## Core Rule

Style labels describe the communication pattern shown in this transcript only. A person may use different styles in different contexts.

## Style Mapping

### Analytical

Behavioral signals:

- Asks for evidence, data, definitions, or assumptions.
- Slows decisions until facts are clear.
- Focuses on accuracy, process, risk, and tradeoffs.
- Uses precise or conditional language.

Possible needs:

- Clear criteria
- Data quality
- Time to evaluate
- Specific next steps

Risk when unsupported:

- May appear hesitant or overly detailed.

### Driver

Behavioral signals:

- Pushes for decisions, speed, ownership, and outcomes.
- Cuts through detail to identify action.
- Challenges ambiguity or delay.
- Uses direct language.

Possible needs:

- Clear authority
- Fast options
- Decision-ready summaries
- Accountability

Risk when unsupported:

- May compress discussion before concerns are fully explored.

### Amiable

Behavioral signals:

- Looks for agreement, inclusion, and relationship stability.
- Softens disagreement.
- Checks comfort, impact, or team implications.
- Uses collaborative language.

Possible needs:

- Trust
- Participation
- Clear interpersonal expectations
- Low-friction follow-up

Risk when unsupported:

- May leave disagreement implicit.

### Expressive

Behavioral signals:

- Uses stories, vision, possibilities, or energetic framing.
- Connects ideas quickly.
- Emphasizes momentum, meaning, or stakeholder reaction.
- May move between topics rapidly.

Possible needs:

- Room to frame the bigger picture
- Visible enthusiasm
- Clear synthesis
- Follow-through structure

Risk when unsupported:

- May leave execution details underdefined.

## Output Requirements

For each style observation:

- Cite transcript behavior.
- Name the style as a situational pattern.
- State confidence.
- Explain how to communicate effectively with that pattern.
- Avoid personality labels such as "is an Analytical person."

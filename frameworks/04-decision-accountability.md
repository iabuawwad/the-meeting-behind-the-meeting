# 04 - Decision Accountability

Use this framework to assess whether the meeting produced decisions and actions that can be executed.

## Decision Quality

Assess each decision using:

- Clarity: Is the decision stated plainly?
- Authority: Did the right person or group approve it?
- Rationale: Is the reason or tradeoff visible?
- Scope: Does the decision define what is included and excluded?
- Dependencies: Are prerequisites or blockers named?
- Reversibility: Is it clear whether the decision is final, temporary, or experimental?
- Evidence: Is the decision supported by transcript facts?

Decision quality levels:

- Strong: clear decision, owner, rationale, and next step.
- Adequate: decision is clear but one execution detail is missing.
- Weak: decision is implied or lacks authority, rationale, or execution detail.
- Not a decision: discussion, preference, or suggestion only.

## Action Quality

Assess each action item using:

- Verb: What action will be taken?
- Owner: Who is responsible?
- Deadline: By when?
- Output criteria: What will be delivered?
- Dependency: What must happen first?
- Confirmation channel: Where will completion be visible?

Action quality levels:

- Strong: owner, deadline, output, and dependency are clear.
- Adequate: owner and output are clear, but timing or dependency is missing.
- Weak: task is mentioned but not assigned or measurable.
- Missing: no actionable next step exists.

## Owner, Deadline, Output Criteria

Every material action should be rewritten as:

```text
Owner will deliver output by deadline, with dependency or confirmation channel if relevant.
```

If any field is missing, mark it explicitly:

- Owner: missing
- Deadline: missing
- Output criteria: missing

## Accountability Gaps

Flag gaps when:

- A decision has no implementation owner.
- An owner is named without a deadline.
- A deadline exists without a defined output.
- Multiple owners are assigned without a single accountable person.
- Follow-up depends on someone absent from the meeting.
- Risk is acknowledged but not assigned.
- The group agrees conceptually but avoids the next operational step.

For each gap, provide:

- Evidence
- Why it matters
- Confidence
- Recommended action

# The Meeting Behind the Meeting

Use this skill for transcripts, meeting notes, conversation threads, interviews, voice notes, minutes, action items, decisions, and risks.

## Mandatory First Step

Run the transcript quality gate before any substantive analysis:

- `frameworks/00-transcript-quality-gate.md`
- Output template: `templates/00-quality-assessment.md`

If the quality gate says to stop, do not continue into interpretation.

## Book Summary References

Use `resources/book-summaries/` as supporting lenses. Do not quote long sections. Do not reproduce book content in outputs. Convert reference material into evidence-based analysis.

Always separate fact, signal, interpretation, alternative explanation, confidence, and recommended action.

Use references and templates only as needed. Keep facts separate from interpretation, include alternative explanations for advanced claims, and avoid certainty about private intent.
